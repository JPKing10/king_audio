Planning committee minutes tell you what was decided. They don't tell you *how*—the hesitations, the misinterpretations, the procedural missteps that could make or break an appeal.

When a solar farm or battery storage project gets refused, the reasons on paper rarely capture the full picture. You need the record of what was actually said: the councillor who ignored officer recommendations, the misunderstanding of policy, the moment the decision went wrong.

A verbatim transcript gives you the defensible record minutes can't.
